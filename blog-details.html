Mastering the Snatch: 5 Drills for Explosive Power

person Coach Mike
calendar_today Oct 24, 2023

Reading Progress

35%

The snatch is often called the king of the Olympic lifts. It requires a unique blend of strength, flexibility, coordination, and raw explosive power. For many CrossFitters, it’s the most technical hurdle they face.

The Foundation of Explosive Hip Extension

Most athletes fail their snatch because they aren't patient enough with the second pull. The hip extension is where the magic happens. Without full extension, you're relying on your arms to muscle the weight up, which limits your PR potential and risks injury.

Fig 1.1: Achieving full vertical extension before the drop.

Drill 1: The Snatch Balance

This is the ultimate drill for confidence under the bar. Starting with the bar on your back, you drop rapidly into a full overhead squat. It teaches you to move your body around the bar rather than moving the bar around your body.

"Speed is the bridge between strength and skill. In the snatch, if you aren't fast, you're heavy."

Drill 2: Tall Snatches

Tall snatches remove the momentum from the first pull. Starting from a standing position with the bar at the hip, you must use only the shrug and the drop to get under the weight. This is perfect for correcting "looping" issues with the barbell.

Remember, consistency beats intensity when learning technique. Strip the weight back, focus on the movement path, and only add kilos when the movement is reflexive.

Getting Started & Memberships

Do I need to be fit to start? expand_more
Absolutely not. CrossFit is infinitely scalable, meaning we adjust every workout to match your current fitness level. We help you get fit; you don't need to be fit to join. Our coaches are trained to modify movements for everyone from professional athletes to grandparents.
What is the On-Ramp program? expand_more
The On-Ramp program is our introductory course designed for beginners. Over three sessions, we teach you the fundamental movements of CrossFit—squats, presses, and deadlifts—ensuring you have the correct technique and safety knowledge before joining our regular group classes.
How much does a membership cost? expand_more
We offer several tiers based on how many times a week you'd like to train. Our most popular 'Unlimited' membership is $180/month. We also have 3-day-a-week options for $140/month. No long-term contracts, just month-to-month commitments to your own health.

Facilities & Coaching

Do you offer drop-ins for visitors? expand_more
Yes! Experienced CrossFitters visiting from out of town are always welcome. Drop-in fee is $25 per class, or you can purchase a weekly pass for $65. Please arrive 15 minutes early to sign a waiver and meet the coach.
What should I bring to my first class? expand_more
Wear comfortable athletic clothing you can move freely in, a pair of supportive sneakers, and bring a water bottle. We have towels and a water refill station available. Most importantly, bring a positive attitude and be ready to work hard.
Are there showers available? expand_more
Yes, we have full-service locker rooms equipped with multiple private showers, hair dryers, and storage lockers. We provide basic toiletries so you can head straight to work or home feeling fresh after your WOD.

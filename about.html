The Journey

Our Story

2014

The Garage Era

We started in a single-car garage with two barbells and a dream. Our founder, Mark Steiner, wanted to create a space where hard work was the only currency. What started as a small group of friends quickly outgrew the driveway.

2018

The Industrial Box

We moved into our first 5,000 sq ft industrial space. With custom rigs and a growing community, we became the premier destination for serious CrossFitters in the city. The community became our core identity.

Today

The Elite Standard

Now operating a state-of-the-art facility, we offer elite coaching, specialized programming, and an unmatched support system. We've helped thousands redefine their limits and change their lives.
